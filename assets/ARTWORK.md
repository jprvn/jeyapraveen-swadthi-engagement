# Original floral assets

Created with the built-in image generator for this invitation, then optimized as WebP.

`floral-arch.webp`: portrait 3D floral arch in a pastel garden, ivory magnolias, blush and apricot roses, lotus flowers, sage eucalyptus, pearl silver filigree and champagne gold ornament; blank central space for the live monogram, soft sunrise light, no printed words or event details.

`floral-bouquet.webp`: transparent foreground arrangement of ivory magnolia, blush and apricot roses, pink lotus, sage eucalyptus, pearl beads, champagne gold and brushed silver leaves; natural soft light, no text or vase.

The PDFs were used only as aesthetic references. Printed event timing was not reused; final owner-confirmed facts remain authoritative.

Music is an original slow pentatonic instrumental synthesized in the browser. It starts only after the guest presses Play music, can be muted, and pauses while the tab is hidden. No music recording, external audio service or credentials are used.
